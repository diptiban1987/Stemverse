## Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Internal Beta (Week 1-2)
1. Deploy to staging environment
2. Internal team testing
3. Bug triage and fixes

### Phase 2: School Pilot (Week 3-4)
1. Onboard 3-5 pilot schools
2. Teacher training sessions
3. Student onboarding with guided tutorials
4. Collect feedback

### Phase 3: Public Beta (Week 5-8)
1. Open registration
2. Free tier available to all
3. Marketplace opens for submissions
4. First Robothrone competition

### Phase 4: Enterprise Pilot (Week 6-10)
1. Onboard 2-3 enterprise clients
2. White-label deployment
3. Custom branding setup
4. SLA and support setup
